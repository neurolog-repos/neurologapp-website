NeuroLog support

How can we help?

For account, app, wearable or study-invitation help, email contact@neurologapp.com. Include your app version and phone model where useful, but do not email passwords, study codes or detailed health information.

Email support

Common topics

Signing in

Use your own NeuroLog account with Apple, Google or an email verification code. If an email code expires, request a new one from the sign-in screen. Never send a password or one-time code to support.

If you previously used Apple or Google, choose the same method again. Contact support before creating a second account if you need to change or link sign-in methods.

Joining a study

Open the invitation link, scan its QR code or enter the code provided by your study team. Review the study details and consent before joining.

If you do not have a code, continue without a study and join later from Settings → Studies. Contact the named study coordinator for a replacement invitation.

Apple Health

On iPhone, review access under Settings → Privacy & Security → Health. A physical iPhone is required for HealthKit syncing.

Health Connect

On Android, open Health Connect → App permissions and select NeuroLog. Health Connect is built into newer Android versions and may need to be installed on older supported devices.

Missing or duplicated data

Connected devices can take time to sync, and the same activity can appear through more than one source. NeuroLog retains source information and selects a canonical source rather than adding duplicates together.

Privacy and account controls

Read the Privacy Policy, manage health permissions in phone settings, or see how to delete an account.

Safety

NeuroLog is not an emergency service.

NeuroLog does not detect or predict events, alert anyone, diagnose conditions or recommend treatment. If you may be experiencing an emergency, call emergency services. In Australia, call 000.

Study participants

Study-specific questions.

For questions about eligibility, consent, study visits, withdrawal or what data your study receives, contact the study team named in the app and your participant information sheet. NeuroLog support can help with the app but cannot make study or clinical decisions.
