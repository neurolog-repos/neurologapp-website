For patients

Understand your own patterns, between visits.

A mobile app for tracking neurological events, symptoms, medication adherence and wearable data. We generate data insights to help people better understand their condition, and support the journey to optimal health.

Log events and symptoms

Record neurological events and symptoms through the NeuroLog app, in the moment or later, with as much or as little detail as you want to give.

Sync your smartwatch

Connect the wearable you already use. Sleep, heart rate and activity then sit alongside the events you log.

Spot patterns over time

See trends in your own data, keep your medication history current, and set medication and logging reminders.
