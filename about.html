About Neurolog

Digitally transforming neurology care.

We are a team of researchers, engineers and entrepreneurs, passionate about using digital health to transform neurology care and generate more objective, better quality data for research. Neurolog Technologies is based in Melbourne, and our team has worked in epilepsy and other episodic neurological conditions, seizure prediction and forecasting, and wearables for more than ten years.
