The problem

Between appointments, nobody can see what is happening.

Epilepsy and other episodic neurological conditions are managed in short, infrequent clinic visits. Clinicians read treatment response and disease progression from patient recall, which is subjective and weighted toward whatever happened most recently.

In between, neurologists have no objective measure of event frequency or treatment efficacy. Patients are in the same position: no way to track their own patterns, and little to engage with until the next appointment.

Neurolog puts patient experience and objective biosignals on the same timeline.

How it works

One continuous record, from the patient's phone to the clinic room.

01

Log and sync, continuously

Patients record events and symptoms, keep their medication list current, and sync a wearable in the background.

02

Build the summary

Reported events sit next to sleep, activity and physiological signals from the same period, ready to review.

03

Review before the visit

Clinicians and researchers open a summary the patient has authorised, and start from evidence rather than recall.
