Supporting research through high quality data curation.

Longitudinal wearable data with timestamped event and symptom records. NeuroLog brings collection, time alignment and export into one workflow, reducing the preparation research teams need to manage.

Discuss a research pilot

Review data across your cohort.

The research dashboard is designed for studies that follow participants over months and years. Tables organised by coded participant IDs bring long-term records together, with individual views for closer review.

Select participants, date ranges and data streams. Check coverage and recording gaps while collection is running, and export the measures and time resolution your analysis needs.

The context behind each measurement.

Data detailIncluded context
Event timing When an event happened and when it was logged, including entries added later.
Dates and frequency Date ranges, time zones, available sampling intervals and any aggregation applied.
Sources and units Measurement sources, units and field definitions, distinguishing wearable data from participant reports.
Completeness Coverage for each participant and data stream, with gaps and missing values visible.
Export settings Selected participants, measures, dates and processing choices, documented with the dataset.

Keep your Python or MATLAB workflow.

Export documented tables for your existing analysis tools. Data curation handles time alignment, consistent fields and source information, so you spend less time joining device files and reconciling columns by hand.

Available measures and time resolution depend on the connected sources. We work with study teams to match collection and export options to their methods.

Consent guides access.

Research participation is a separate choice. Access follows the study's consent and permissions, with coded participant IDs used in research views.
