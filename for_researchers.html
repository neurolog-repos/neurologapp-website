Supporting research through high quality data curation.

NeuroLog makes longitudinal wearable and event data collection easy, leaving you more time for the science. Simply enrol your study participants, and NeuroLog handles the data collection, storage and curation. Access and download your participants' records and insights through a secure web portal, saving you both time and money.

Discuss a research pilot

Review data across your cohort.

The research portal is designed for studies that follow participants from days to years. View your whole cohort at a glance or zoom in on individual participants.

Check study adherence and recording gaps while collection is running, and export the appropriate data for your research study.

Export the data that suits your study and analysis pipeline.

Data type What you can access
Event details Event timing, symptoms, severity and triggers.
Medication Medication type, dosage, frequency and adherence.
Wearable data* Raw wearable data signals, including continuous heart rate, sleep patterns, activity and other metrics.
Mood Daily self-reported mood.

* Wearable data depends on the participant's smartwatch and device type.

Export data in formats that suit your existing analysis tools. Data curation handles time alignment, consistent fields and source information, so you spend less time preprocessing your data.

We work with study teams to match collection and export options to their needs.
